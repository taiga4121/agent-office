# E2E spec: room tiling

## Objective
Validate the project room layout reads as a continuous office floor with wall and floor tile surfaces connected without visible white seams.

## Steps
1. Open the office dashboard.
2. Confirm that each project renders as a room with wall boundaries and a floor interior.
3. Look across the room edges and confirm the wall/floor tiles visually connect without a pale white gap.
4. Switch among project counts (1, 2, 3+) and confirm the same seam-free layout persists.
5. Check that the selected room remains readable while the sidebar chat panel stays fixed to the right.

## Expected result
The room surfaces appear as a single, continuous office scene with no visible white lines or seams between tiled materials.
